# Session 8 Reflection: Multidimensional data
*Date: May 04, 2026 <br>
Course: SpaceMed – U5-EC3 Digital Literacy* <br>
In session 8 we looked at multidimensional data (fMRI), so overall we went from 1D lists to tabular data in pandas and now 4D fMRI scans. We started by reviewing what we learned last time, specifically about writing programs for the command line and the importance of testing our code. Then we started looking at our dataset and understanding its dimensions. We used a library called NiBabel to read neuroimaging data sets. This let us look at "metadata" like the size of the dataset and the size of the individual voxels. We used matplotlib to actually plot slices of the brain anatomy in grayscale so we could see what the data looks like. We then transformed data into 2D (time series) to display curves of functional MRI signal (brain response to stimulus) of three different brain areas. Then we calculated correlation between them to see a significant response vs. noise.
